# AI Proofreader Plugin for Omarchy

Instant AI grammar fix, sentence polisher, tone rewriter, and English translator right from the Omarchy status bar.

## 🚀 Features

- **5 One-Click Polishing Modes**:
  - 🛠️ **Fix**: Fixes grammar, typos, punctuation, and wording while keeping your voice.
  - 👔 **Work**: Polishes for professional emails, PRs, Slack/Teams messages, and documentation.
  - ⚡ **Concise**: Cuts filler words to make sentences crisp and direct.
  - 💬 **Casual**: Warm, friendly, and natural conversational tone.
  - 🌐 **English**: Translates any language (e.g. Indonesian) into natural, fluent English.
- **Auto Clipboard Grabbing**: Automatically fills input with your current clipboard (`wl-paste`).
- **1-Click Copy & Close**: Press `Enter` to copy the polished text to your clipboard (`wl-copy`) and close.
- **Multi-Backend AI Support**:
  - **Google Gemini** (100% free API keys from [Google AI Studio](https://aistudio.google.com/app/apikey))
  - **Groq** (Ultra-fast Llama 3.3)
  - **OpenAI** (ChatGPT / GPT-4o-mini)
  - **Ollama** (100% offline & private local LLMs)

---

## ⌨️ Shortcuts

- **`Ctrl + Enter`**: Proofread / Polish
- **`Esc`**: Close popup
